Tips and Tricks

Investing has become a huge part of my life. My brother, who is an accountant, has paved the way for me. He showed me how to invest and what to invest in, and since then I have been investing in stocks and ETFs weekly. If you want to learn more, I recommend the video below and the app Robinhood.

Look on eBay, Amazon, or Facebook Marketplace for used clubs. Find a local range to practice. I also recommend the app 18 Birdies to track scores and discover new courses.

The Rocket Money app helped me track my spending and cut unnecessary subscriptions. I also keep a fixed amount in my checking account and move the rest to savings.
